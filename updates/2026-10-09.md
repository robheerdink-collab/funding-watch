- All 195 entries converted to the new schema (`config/profile.json`).

  show as "Expected" with month and year only.
- Rolling calls with the 31 Dec placeholder date now have no deadline.
- All 99 closed entries, plus 9 entries still marked Open or Upcoming whose deadline had passed
  (IDs 12, 41, 152, 161, 163, 174, 175, 184, 188), moved to `data/archive.json`.
