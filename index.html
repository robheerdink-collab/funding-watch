    How this page is kept up to date

    Every Saturday morning the list is checked against funders’ official pages: new calls are added, deadlines and budgets corrected, and closed calls moved to the archive. Status and days left are calculated from today’s date when you open the page.

    Dates marked “expected” are based on the previous round and not yet announced. Always check the official call page before you plan an application.
