How this list is kept up to date

Every Saturday morning the list is checked against the funders’ own pages: new calls are added, deadlines, budgets and conditions corrected, and closed calls moved to the archive. Status and days left are calculated from today’s date when you open the page.

“Expected” means a new round is likely but the funder has not announced it; the date is based on the previous round. Always check the official call page before you plan an application.
